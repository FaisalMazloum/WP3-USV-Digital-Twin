## 🎬 Simulation Showcases

Short demonstrations of the **REMORA Simulator** (Unity) and of swarm behaviours from the **ROS2Swarm** framework running in it. ROS2Swarm runs as ROS 2 nodes and controls the simulated robots through the ROS-TCP bridge.

### 🏛️ REMORA Simulator Overview
A short demonstration of the REMORA simulator. The simulator models buoyancy physics, wind, waves and wake generation.
